### Three Interactive Payment Physics Simulators Included
1. **64-Bit ISO 8583 Primary Bitmap Decoder (Ch. 4–5)**:
   - Decode `0100` Authorization Requests, `0110` Responses, `0200` Single-Message Financial Presentments, `0400` Reversals, and `0420` Stand-In Reversal Advices across active Data Elements (`DE 2` PAN/Token, `DE 3` Processing Code, `DE 4` Minor-Unit Amount, `DE 11` STAN, `DE 18` MCC, `DE 37` RRN, `DE 39` Response Code, `DE 55` EMV TLV Cryptograms).
2. **Merchant Acquiring Fee Waterfall: IC++ vs. Blended Flat-Rate Simulator (Ch. 6)**:
   - Model monthly and annual cost deltas across Issuer Interchange, Card Network Assessments, and Acquirer Markup (`IC++`) versus Payment Facilitator (`PayFac`) blended flat-rate pricing across regulated debit, consumer rewards credit, and commercial cards.
3. **Wholesale RTGS Liquidity-Saving Netting & Atomic FX PvP Exposure Calculator (Ch. 13–15)**:
   - Simulate intraday central bank reserve requirements under pure Real-Time Gross Settlement (`RTGS`) versus multilateral liquidity-saving algorithms (e.g., `26:1` gridlock compression) and quantify bilateral Herstatt settlement principal risk eliminated via Payment-versus-Payment (`PvP`).

## Companion Book Overview (*The Physics of Payments*)
- **Format Matrix**: Kindle eBook, `7" × 10"` Paperback (`242` pages), and `7" × 10"` Case-Laminate Hardcover (`20` chapters across `6` architectural movements, illustrated with `20` monochrome architectural schematics).
- **The Three Laws of Payment Physics**:
  1. **Law I — The Speed-Finality Divide**: Authorization messaging moves at fiber-optic speed; irrevocable settlement occurs only when commercial or central bank balance sheets mutate under statutory finality.
  2. **Law II — The Conservation of Friction**: Friction removed at checkout relocates into hardware cryptography, issuer underwriting, pre-funded liquidity pools, or post-settlement dispute arbitration.
  3. **Law III — The Conservation of Risk**: Credit, liquidity, fraud, FX principal, and operational risks never vanish; every rail prices and allocates them across payers, merchants, switches, commercial banks, and central banks.
